ABOUT

Ekuivalen

I’m Ekuivalen Muhammad Firdaus, though most people call me Valen. I'm a college student living in Bogor, Indonesia studying software engineering while spending my days exploring creative storytelling, staying active, and learning from new experiences.

I created this website as my own personal space to introduce myself and keep an archive of my journey. Beyond the projects I build, this is where I document campus life, outdoor adventures, and honest reflections picked up along the way.

More than chasing perfection, I value staying curious and showing up consistently. Whether I’m picking up a new skill, editing a short vlog, or heading outdoors with friends, I enjoy turning ordinary days into stories worth remembering.

What keeps me busy

  • Growing through my studies, coursework, and creative projects at Politeknik IDN.
  • Filming and editing short-form videos to capture daily moments and personal milestones.
  • Practicing English and writing regularly to share ideas more clearly and confidently.
  • Taking on real-world challenges that push me to adapt, collaborate, and think resourcefully.

Beyond the screen

  • Staying energized with endurance sports—morning jogs with friends, cycling, and swimming.
  • Hiking through hills and mountains whenever I need fresh air and a clear head.
  • Following Formula 1 races closely and unwinding with a good horror movie or pop music.

PERSONAL MINDSET

I believe real growth comes from daily consistency, staying humble, and treating every mistake as part of the learning process. Building good work matters—but so do the people you meet, the stories you collect, and the balance you keep along the way.
